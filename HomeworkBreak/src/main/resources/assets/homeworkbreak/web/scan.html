Homework Break

Scan Answers & Print

1. Take a picture of your homework

Hold the sheet flat and close to the webcam with good light. You can also upload a photo instead, for example one from your phone.

2. Check the questions

The scanner isn't perfect, so fix any mistakes. Untick anything that isn't a question, or use "Join with above" when one question got split into two.

3. Send them to Minecraft

They'll pop up one at a time while you play. Each one has a box for your answer and a drawing area for working it out.
